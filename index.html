📣 Marketing campaigns

A campaign is saved automatically when you generate captions for a product. You get a reminder every day of the campaign, and an alarm when it ends.

🔊 Alarm settings

While the app is open (or in the background on your phone), reminders ring, vibrate and show a notification. A web app cannot ring when it is fully closed, so for important dates also tap Add to Google Calendar or Phone calendar: your calendar will then alarm even when this app is closed.

Contact details

Posters show only WhatsApp and Website. Captions use only the Page name and WhatsApp number (no links), plus a line saying the WhatsApp link is in the comments.

After changing these, open a product and tap "Generate captions" again.

Product details for captions

Fill only what is true. "All products (default)" applies to every product. Pick one product to write its own details, which replace the default for that product. A caption mentions packages, activation, delivery or support only when it is written here, in your own words. Empty field = nothing is said about it. Do not write prices. Windows License Key does not use the default; give it its own details if you want them.

Product logos

Upload each product logo once (PNG with transparent background works best). Logos stay on this phone and are used on the posters.

My own products

Add new products here, no code changes needed. Write the product details in Bengali (they go into the caption). No prices or links.

Backup

Save settings, your own products, campaigns and reminders to a file. Use it to restore after changing phone or clearing browser data.
