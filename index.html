📣 Marketing campaigns

A campaign is saved automatically when you generate captions for a product. You get a reminder every day of the campaign, and an alarm when it ends.

🔊 Alarm settings

While the app is open (or in the background on your phone), reminders ring, vibrate and show a notification. A web app cannot ring when it is fully closed, so for important dates also tap Add to Google Calendar or Phone calendar: your calendar will then alarm even when this app is closed.

Contact details

Captions use only the Page name and WhatsApp number (no links), plus a line saying the WhatsApp link is in the comments.

After changing these, open a product and tap "Generate captions" again.

Product details for captions

Fill only what is true. "All products (default)" applies to every product. Pick one product to write its own details, which replace the default for that product. A caption mentions packages, activation, delivery or support only when it is written here, in your own words. Empty field = nothing is said about it. Do not write prices. Windows License Key takes only the activation line (Personal/Shared) from the default; give it its own details if you want more.

My own products

Add new products here, no code changes needed. Write the product details in Bengali (they go into the caption). No prices or links.

Google account

Sign in once to (1) add reminders straight into Google Calendar with one tap and (2) keep your settings, own products, campaigns and reminders backed up in your own Google Drive, so nothing is lost if you clear the browser or change phone. The backup sits in a hidden app folder that only this tool can open.

Calendar events appear as a phone notification at the set time. They are not a ringing alarm. For ringing, set an alarm in the Clock app. Google asks you to sign in again about once an hour; the tool then continues syncing.

Backup

Save settings, your own products, campaigns and reminders to a file. Use it to restore after changing phone or clearing browser data.
